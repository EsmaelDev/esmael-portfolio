## Before publishing commercially

Some images originate from editorial or retail sources where reuse licensing is
unclear. For a personal, educational portfolio they serve as clearly-labelled
placeholders — but **verify licensing or swap in your own photos/screenshots**
before any commercial use. The fastest path: replace all six with real
screenshots of your three projects (instructions in the README, section 7).
